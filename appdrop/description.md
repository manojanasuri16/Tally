Track what your family owes and lends, and keep everyone in the family on the same page.

Tally is a simple shared book for family money: EMIs, credit cards, pledged gold, and money taken from or given to people you know.

- See what's still to pay, what's due this month (with the month's total) and what's overdue
- Bank EMIs (reducing or flat), interest only (every month, 3 or 6 months, or a year), no interest, or fixed instalments like ₹1,000 a week
- Credit cards: outstanding, minimum due, pay-by date and credit limit, including limits shared between cards
- Set the first payment date, so payments can fall on a different day from the day the money came
- Log each payment with the date, plus a bank SMS, photo or document if you like
- Share with family: say whose each entry is, and switch between Everyone and Mine
- Deleted by mistake? Entries stay in Recently deleted for 30 days
- Telugu, Hindi and English, with an Easy mode for bigger text and fewer choices
- Payment reminders and a home-screen widget

**Private by design: your family's book stays on your phone, and sharing is end-to-end encrypted.**
No account, no ads, no tracking. Mark any entry private and it never leaves your phone.

**Why no account?** Tally has no sign-up, email or password. Your family's book is end-to-end encrypted, so even the server can't read it, and nobody can take over your account, because there isn't one. Moving to a new phone? A family member lets you back in, and your backup brings back anything you kept private.
